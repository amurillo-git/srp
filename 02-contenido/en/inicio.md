# Experience Off-Road Thrills at Sarapiquí Race Park

270cc go karts on a 500-meter track. Extreme fun for the whole family.

## Who We Are

Sarapiquí Race Park is the number-one adventure destination in Horquetas de Sarapiquí, Costa Rica. We offer next-generation go kart racing experiences designed for beginners and experienced drivers alike.

Located on Ruta 4 in Horquetas de Sarapiquí, we're easy to find and accessible from every corner of the region.

[IMAGE: go-kart-270cc-salpicando-lodo-pista-off-road-sarapiqui | alt: Go kart 270cc spraying mud across the off-road track at Sarapiquí Race Park]

## Signature Activities

Feel the adrenaline in our 270cc karts on an off-road track of approximately 500 meters. The experience is perfect for the whole family:

- **Next-generation karts:** 270cc engines, professional racing design, and state-of-the-art safety protection
- **Challenging terrain:** ~500 meters with technical curves, straightaways, and varied surfaces
- **For everyone:** minimum age 9 to drive solo; younger children can ride with an adult
- **Complete services:** safety briefing, protective gear, track access, shower facilities
