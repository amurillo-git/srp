# Frequently Asked Questions – Sarapiquí Race Park

Here you'll find answers to the most common questions. If you have additional concerns, **contact us on WhatsApp at +506 7210-0389 or by email at info@srp.cr**.

---

## Ages and Requirements

### What's the minimum age to drive a go kart?

The minimum age to drive a go kart solo is **9 years**. Children under 9 can participate in the same go kart with an adult or responsible guardian.

### Is there a weight limit?

Yes. Maximum weight is:
- **100 kg for adults (220 lbs)**
- **50 kg for children (110 lbs)**

The driver must also be tall enough to comfortably reach the kart's pedals.

### Do I need special physical fitness?

Good physical condition is recommended. No specific restrictions beyond being able to reach pedals and control the kart safely.

## Booking and Payments

### Is advance booking required?

**Yes**, advance booking is mandatory. Advance booking is necessary for your convenience: this way you don't have to wait. You arrive at your reserved time and go straight onto the track.

### Can I show up without an advance reservation?

**No.** Advance booking is required — we cannot accommodate walk-ins without a reservation. Please contact us ahead of time to secure your spot.

### What deposit is required?

A **50% deposit of the total** reservation amount is required to confirm. The deposit applies to your package cost.

### What's the cancellation policy?

- **48 hours or more in advance:** full deposit refund.
- **Less than 48 hours before your reserved time:** deposit is **non-refundable**.

## Operating Hours

### What are the operating hours?

We're open:
- **Saturdays: 9:00 am – 4:00 pm**
- **Sundays: 9:00 am – 4:00 pm**
- **National holidays: 9:00 am – 4:00 pm**

**Closed Monday through Friday.**

### What happens if it rains?

It doesn't matter if it rains — we operate the same. If a road closure occurs due to severe weather, your reservation is not lost: we'll reschedule you within the following **30 days**.

## How to Get Here

### How long does it take to arrive from different cities?

From major Costa Rica destinations:

| City/Area | Approximate Time |
|---|---|
| San José | ~1.5 hours |
| Puerto Viejo de Sarapiquí | ~15 minutes |
| La Fortuna | ~1 hour 30 minutes |
| Guápiles | ~30 minutes |
| Cartago | ~2 hours |

### Can I get there in any type of vehicle?

**Yes**, you can arrive in any vehicle. We're located at **Horquetas de Sarapiquí, on Ruta 4, kilometer 16, Costa Rica** — a main highway with easy access.

### Can I use public transportation?

**Yes**, public transportation is available. From **San José or Guápiles**, take the **Los Caribeños** buses heading to Puerto Viejo de Sarapiquí. Ask the driver to let you know at the stop **before Materiales Sarapiquí**. For exact schedules, **contact us on WhatsApp at +506 7210-0389 or by email at info@srp.cr**.

## Pricing and Packages

### What's the price of a single lap?

A single lap costs **₡2,000**.

### What packages are available?

We offer these packages:

**Individual:**
- **Single lap:** ₡2,000
- **10-minute package:** ₡6,000 (~5-6 laps) — Most Popular
- **15-minute package:** ₡7,000 (~8-9 laps) — Maximum Adrenaline

**For groups (minimum 5 people, maximum 5 per heat):**
- **Group 10-minute:** ₡5,500 per person
- **Group 15-minute:** ₡6,500 per person
- **Groups 20+ people:** inquire about additional discounts

### What's included in the price?

Your package price includes:
- 270cc go kart racing
- Pre-race safety briefing
- Protective gear (helmet, harness, etc.)
- Off-road track access
- Shower facilities

### Is food available at the park?

**Yes**, food is available. We have **agreements with local restaurants** who deliver orders to our facilities. You can enjoy a great meal between races.

### What's NOT included in the price?

Not included:
- Food or beverages (though we have restaurant partnerships available — ask us)
- Professional photographs

## Booking Process

### How do I make a reservation?

The process is simple:

1. **Contact us** on WhatsApp at +506 7210-0389 or by email at info@srp.cr
2. **Specify** the date, time, and number of people coming
3. **Choose your package** (single lap, 10-minute, or 15-minute)
4. **Make the 50% deposit** to confirm your reservation
5. **Confirmed!** You'll receive final confirmation
