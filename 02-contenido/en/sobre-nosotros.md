# About Us – Sarapiquí Race Park

## Who We Are

At Sarapiquí Race Park, we are a family business passionate about creating extraordinary experiences. We stand out with powerful go karts and an exciting off-road track, designed to experience speed, overcome challenges, and enjoy maximum adrenaline. Here, every lap becomes an unforgettable adventure to share with family and friends.

## Our Commitment

Located in the heart of Horquetas de Sarapiquí, on Ruta 4, we focus on providing safe, thrilling, and inclusive experiences for visitors of all ages and skill levels. From the youngest drivers accompanied by an adult to experienced pilots seeking maximum adrenaline, everyone finds their ideal experience in our park.
