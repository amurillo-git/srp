# Pricing and Packages – Sarapiquí Race Park

## Individual Rates

Perfect if you're coming solo or with someone to take turns:

- **Single Lap:** ₡2,000 per lap
- **10-Minute Package:** ₡6,000 (~5-6 laps) — Most Popular
- **15-Minute Package:** ₡7,000 (~8-9 laps) — Maximum Adrenaline

## Group Packages

Bring your friends! We offer special rates for groups:

- **Minimum:** 5 people
- **Maximum per heat:** 5 people per race

### Group Pricing

- **Group 10-Minute:** ₡5,500 per person (~5-6 laps)
- **Group 15-Minute:** ₡6,500 per person (~8-9 laps)

### Special Discounts

- **Groups of 20+ people:** additional discount available (inquire directly)

## What's Included

Every Sarapiquí Race Park package includes:

- 270cc go kart racing
- Pre-race safety briefing
- Protective gear (helmet, harness, protections)
- Full access to the 500-meter off-road track
- Shower facilities

## What's NOT Included

- Food or beverages
- Professional photographs (bring your phone for your own photos)

## Terms and Conditions

### Deposit

- **Required:** 50% of total amount to confirm your reservation
- **Balance:** remaining 50% paid upon arrival

### Cancellation

- **Cancellation 48 hours or more in advance:** full deposit refund
- **Cancellation less than 48 hours in advance:** deposit is non-refundable

## Age and Weight Requirements

Verify you meet our requirements before booking:

- **Minimum age:** 9 years to drive solo
- **Children under 9:** can ride with an adult
- **Maximum weight:** 100 kg (adults / 220 lbs), 50 kg (children / 110 lbs)

For complete details on safety restrictions, [check our activities section](/en/activities/).

## Operating Hours

- **Saturday:** 9:00 am – 4:00 pm
- **Sunday:** 9:00 am – 4:00 pm
- **Holidays:** 9:00 am – 4:00 pm
- **Rest of week:** Closed

**Advance booking required.** Contact us to confirm availability.
