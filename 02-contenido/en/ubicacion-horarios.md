# Location and Hours – Sarapiquí Race Park

## Where to Find Us

**Address:**  
Horquetas de Sarapiquí, Ruta 4, kilometer 16, Costa Rica

[EMBEDDED MAP: https://maps.app.goo.gl/Rk2z39BCwLQFtY4A9]

[IMAGE: entrada-rotulo-sarapiqui-race-park-pilotos | alt: Two visitors in racing helmets with go karts stand in front of Sarapiquí Race Park entrance sign]

## How to Get Here

We're located on Ruta 4 (the main highway), easily accessible from across the region:

- **From San José:** ~1.5 hours
- **From Puerto Viejo:** ~15 minutes
- **From La Fortuna:** ~1 hour 30 minutes
- **From Guápiles:** ~30 minutes
- **From Cartago:** ~2 hours

You can reach us by any type of vehicle.

### By Public Transportation

From **San José or Guápiles**, you can take the **Los Caribeños** buses heading to Puerto Viejo de Sarapiquí. Ask the driver to let you know at the stop **before Materiales Sarapiquí**. For exact bus schedules, contact us.
