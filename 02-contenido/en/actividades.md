# Go Kart Racing at Sarapiquí Race Park

Discover all the details about our off-road races, karts, track, and what you need to know to participate.

## The Best Go Karts in Costa Rica

Our go karts are engineered to guarantee safety, performance, and equal-opportunity fun.

### Technical Specifications

- **Engine:** 270cc power
- **Design:** Professional racing with robust frame
- **Size and comfort:** Built for safe piloting
- **Protection:** Latest-generation harnesses and safety gear
- **Maintenance:** Regular and rigorous

[IMAGE: piloto-cubierto-lodo-kart-frente-instalaciones | alt: Mud-covered driver controls go kart in front of Sarapiquí Race Park facilities]

## Professional Off-Road Track

Our track measures approximately 500 meters and combines the best of off-road karting:

- Technical curves that challenge your skill
- Straights where you reach top speed
- Varied terrain with different surface types
- Mud spray and pure adrenaline

[IMAGE: salpicadura-lodo-alta-velocidad-go-kart | alt: Mud spray at high speed during off-road go kart race at Sarapiquí Race Park]

## Requirements and Restrictions

Before you drive, verify that you meet our safety requirements:

- **Minimum age:** 9 years to drive solo
- **Children under 9:** can ride with an adult
- **Maximum weight:** 100 kg (adults / 220 lbs), 50 kg (children / 110 lbs)
- **Physical condition:** must be able to operate controls and reach pedals
- **Height restriction:** ability to reach pedals and control the vehicle

If you have questions about whether you meet the requirements, [check our pricing and terms](/en/pricing/) or contact us directly.

[IMAGE: piloto-cruza-charco-lodo-go-kart-velocidad | alt: Driver crosses mud puddle at high speed in go kart, creating a strong spray]

## Safety

Your safety is our highest priority.

- **Mandatory safety briefing:** all drivers receive instruction before each session
- **Certified staff:** supervises at all times during your race
- **Protective equipment:** helmet, harness, and protections included

[IMAGE: kart-estructura-roja-salpicadura-lodo-curva | alt: Red-framed go kart raises mud while driver takes curve on the off-road track]

## Varied Terrain

Forget flat tracks. Our circuit includes:

- Deep mud puddles that test your control
- Compacted dirt surfaces with different textures
- Full nature immersion: forest, stacked tires as track markers, 100% off-road environment

[IMAGE: piloto-cruza-pozo-lodo-profundo-kart-off-road | alt: Driver traverses deep mud puddle in go kart, with stacked tires marking track boundaries]
